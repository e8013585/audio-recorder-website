Built for people who care about the recording

Every control here changes the audio that lands on your device — nothing is decorative.

🎚️

Ten formats, properly

WAV in 16-bit or 32-bit float, FLAC, MP3, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, AMR and 3GP. Sample rate up to 96 kHz and bit rate up to 320 kbps, with only the values each format genuinely supports offered.

📈

A waveform you can read

Drawn one hairline per pixel with a time scale above and below, so the peaks line up with the moments that made them. Colour and grid are yours to set.

🔇

Skip silence and voice activation

Drop silent stretches as you record, or capture only while someone is speaking. Both have adjustable thresholds, and the timer counts only what was kept.

✂️

Trim, fade and convert

Edit on the device. Trimming copies the stream rather than re-encoding, so a 320 kbps file stays 320 kbps. Convert between any of the supported formats.

🎙️

Real capture controls

Gain from −30 to +30 dB, noise suppression, automatic gain control, microphone selection and direction, mono or stereo, and monitoring through headphones while you record.

📞

Survives real life

Pause or stop automatically on an incoming call. Recordings are written to private storage first and recovered after a crash, so an interrupted session is not a lost one.

🔒

Locked to you

Optional PIN or biometric unlock, re-armed whenever the app leaves the screen. PINs are salted and hashed, never stored as text.

🌐

Copy files over Wi-Fi

Turn on the built-in server and download recordings from a browser on the same network. Password protected, and off unless you start it.

🏷️

Names that make sense

Build the filename from the parts you want — date, time, milliseconds, prefix — in the order you want, in your own time zone. Rename on save if you prefer.

What the app supports, in full.

Bit rates 8–320 kbps for compressed formats. Lossless formats report their measured rate instead
Channels Mono or stereo
Microphone Default, camcorder, voice recognition, unprocessed or voice-communication source; front, rear or external direction; Bluetooth headset input
Processing Gain −30 to +30 dB, noise suppression, automatic gain control, silence skipping, voice activation
Editing Trim, fade in, fade out, format conversion
Playback Variable speed, ten-second skips, seek by tapping the waveform, background playback with notification controls
Organisation Search, rename, share, delete, audio profiles you can save and reload
Interface Light, dark or system theme, Material You dynamic colour, two design styles, home-screen recording widget
Languages 125 languages, plus 18 numbering systems including Eastern Arabic, Devanagari, Thai, Bengali, Tamil and Khmer

Your recordings stay on your device

This is the whole story, and the privacy policy says the same thing at greater length.

🚫

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

There is no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics of any kind. The app contains no tracking libraries.

📴

Nothing is uploaded

Recordings are never sent anywhere. The only network feature is the Wi-Fi server you switch on yourself, which serves files on your local network and nowhere else.

📍

Location is optional

If you enable location tagging, an approximate place name is stored with the recording on your device. Leave it off and no location is ever requested.
